# Opening hours: Peten rauta ja romu

Fictional store. All hours and dates below are invented for this course project.

## Regular hours (Finnish time)

| Day | Hours |
|---|---|
| Monday to Friday | 10:00 to 21:00 |
| Saturday | 10:00 to 18:00 |
| Sunday | 12:00 to 18:00 |

## Public holidays

The store is closed all day on public holidays. A holiday overrides the regular hours of the weekday it falls on.

Closed days in the test period (store policy for this exercise):

| Date | Weekday | Day | Hours |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6 December 2026 | Sunday | Independence Day | Closed |
| 24 December 2026 | Thursday | Christmas Eve (store decision) | Closed |
| 25 December 2026 | Friday | Christmas Day | Closed |
| 26 December 2026 | Saturday | Boxing Day | Closed |
| 1 January 2027 | Friday | New Year's Day | Closed |
| 6 January 2027 | Wednesday | Epiphany | Closed |

All other days follow the regular hours.

## Notes for staff

- The days before and after a holiday follow the regular hours unless listed above.
- Opening hours for days after 6 January 2027 are not in this document. If asked, the answer is: regular hours apply unless a holiday notice is published, and staff should check.
