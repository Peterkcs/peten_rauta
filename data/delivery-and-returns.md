# Delivery and returns: Peten rauta ja romu

Fictional store. All rules below are invented for this course project.

## Stock statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| SHELF | In the store. Can be bought today during opening hours. |
| WAREHOUSE | In our warehouse. Can usually be in the store for pickup within 1 working day. |
| ORDER | Not kept in stock. Ordered from the supplier. The delivery time in the stock list is in working days, counted from order confirmation, to the store. |
| SOLD_OUT | Temporarily sold out. A restock is expected but its date is not confirmed. |

Working days are Monday to Friday, excluding the public holidays in the opening hours document.

## Delivery

- Delivery times are estimates. They are never guaranteed dates.
- Home delivery adds 1 to 2 working days to the delivery time to the store. The delivery fee is shown when ordering.
- Example: an ORDER item with 10 to 14 working days to the store takes 11 to 16 working days to a home address.

## Returns (for reference only)

Version 1 of the assistant does not answer returns questions. The text is here so the assistant can recognise what it must pass to a person.

- Unused products in their original packaging can be returned within 30 days of purchase with the receipt.
- Exceptions to the return period are decided only by the customer service lead.
- Refunds and compensation are handled only by the customer service lead and finance.
