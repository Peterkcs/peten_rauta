Peten rauta ja romu

Email triage and reply-drafting assistant for a fictional hardware store. The assistant answers only three routine questions and sends everything else to a named owner. A staff member sends every reply.

Overview

Routine, drafted

  • Opening hours, including holidays
  • Stock status of a named product
  • Delivery time of an order-only item

Not routine, escalated

  • Returns, complaints, warranty
  • Prices, discounts, invoices
  • Product advice and technical questions
  • Any email that mixes routine and non-routine

Human in the loop

  • Output has a fixed block with a VERIFY line
  • Email text is treated as data, not instructions
  • Staff checks and sends every reply

How it works

  1. Input. Staff pastes a customer email into the tool.
  2. Triage. The assistant sets CATEGORY to ROUTINE or NOT_ROUTINE and gives a short REASON.
  3. Draft or route. Routine: a draft built only from the documents. Not routine: ESCALATE_TO names an owner and no draft is written.
  4. Check. VERIFY lists what staff must confirm, for example the stock status on the shelf.

Output block

CATEGORY: ROUTINE | NOT_ROUTINE
REASON: one sentence
ESCALATE_TO: none | customer_service_lead | store_manager | product_advisor | finance
DRAFT: reply in the customer's language, or none
SOURCES: document and row used
VERIFY: what staff must confirm before sending

Evaluation plan

20 invented test emails with the expected result written before any run. Automatic checks cover format, category, owner and simple rules on the draft. A person judges the facts in each draft. The costliest error is a missed escalation, so escalation recall is reported first.

About

Fictional store, no affiliation. "Peten rauta ja romu" is invented for a university course. It is not connected to K-Rauta, Kesko or any other company. This site uses no logos, names, images or texts from any retailer.

Colours. The blue, yellow and near-black are approximate values taken from a third-party colour listing, not from official brand guidelines. They are used here as a colour scheme only.

Chat demo. The "Kokeile chättiä" tab runs a rule-based engine in the browser by default. It is not an AI model and its outputs are not results of the assistant under test. With apiUrl set in config.js the tab calls your own test endpoint instead.

Data. All emails, names, stock rows and hours are synthetic. Do not paste real customer data into this prototype.

AI use. Parts of this repository were drafted with an AI assistant and reviewed by the group. Results are never generated by AI and presented as measured.
